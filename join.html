Leadership

Board & associate positions

Once a year, a small group of members steps up to run ATS. There aren't many seats and applications are competitive, mostly because a semester on the board is worth competing for.

The return

What a board seat gives you

01

Leadership you can prove

You'll run firm nights start to finish: planning, budget, promotion, and follow-up. When an interviewer asks for an example, you'll have a real one.

02

Recruiters on a first-name basis

Board members work directly with the professionals other students only meet at a career fair. Those relationships carry into recruiting season.

03

Skills the job actually requires

Budgets, timelines, delegating to people, writing like a professional. You practice all of it every week on something real, not on a group project.
