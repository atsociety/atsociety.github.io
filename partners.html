How it plays out

A semester of partnership, start to finish

We try to run events the way you'd run an engagement: planned ahead, owned by someone specific, and followed up on. Here's how a semester with us actually goes.

  • Before the semester

    Your event goes into our semester plan with a locked date, a reserved room, and a named owner on our board. You get one point of contact from day one.

  • Two to three weeks out

    You get your logistics one-pager covering parking, the room, the run-of-show, timing, and expected headcount. Promotion builds from there: we announce your event from the front at the meeting the week before, then post it across GroupMe, Instagram, and the site about a week out with the RSVP link.

  • Event night

    We get there 45 minutes early. One board member is responsible for your guests from the door onward: setup, introductions, attendance, photos. That leaves you free to do the only part we can't do for you, which is talk to students.

  • Within 48 hours

    You get one email with the full debrief: headcount, what students said on the feedback form, and photos from the night. Every time, not just when it went well. It's written into our operating manual as a rule.

  • All semester long

    Your logo stays on the site and on our event slides, your internships stay posted with direct apply links, and the board answers your emails within two business days during the school year. Usually the same day.
